# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically constructive
community. Participants should be able to question evidence, methods, assumptions,
and project decisions without attacking people or identities.

## Expected behavior

- be specific about evidence and uncertainty;
- distinguish disagreement from misconduct;
- welcome contributors across disciplines and career stages;
- give credit for code, cases, reviews, and scientific expertise;
- protect unpublished, identifying, and confidential information;
- document conflicts of interest in benchmark or rule adjudication;
- accept that “unknown” and “experts disagree” can be valid outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private or unpublished data;
- fabricating evidence, reviews, benchmark labels, or contributor identities;
- pressuring reviewers to weaken a rule for a preferred scientific conclusion;
- undisclosed promotion of a model, journal, institution, or commercial service;
- retaliation against good-faith scientific criticism or security reporting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
published contact channel. Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality as far
as practical, document decisions, and recuse themselves when conflicted.

Possible responses include clarification, warning, removal of content, temporary
restriction, or permanent exclusion. Decisions may be appealed to an unconflicted
maintainer; a broader governance process will replace this mechanism as the project
grows.

This policy is adapted in spirit from common open-source community standards while
emphasizing scientific criticism, provenance, privacy, and conflicts of interest.
